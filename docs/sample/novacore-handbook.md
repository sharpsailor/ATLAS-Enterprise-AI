# NovaCore Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Carry forward
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year.
Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00, when everyone is expected to be reachable.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work from outside the country requires written approval from HR and IT Security.

## IT and security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal.
Report suspected security incidents to security@novacore.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Travel and reimbursement
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
Expense claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the expense date.
